Flappy Bevy Privacy Policy

Updated: September 29, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS game Flappy Bevy.

Game progress

The game does not require an account. High scores, collection unlocks, selected items, and sound settings are stored on your device. The game developer does not operate an account or game server for this progress. Removing the app removes its locally stored game data.

If you sign in to Game Center, the game sends your highest score to Apple's Game Center leaderboard. Game Center sign-in is optional. Apple manages Game Center data under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The game uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner ads. Google's ad SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and app interactions to deliver and measure ads and maintain the service. Where permitted by consent and device settings, device identifiers may be used for cross-app ad targeting and measurement. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's app data disclosure guidance.

The game uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather and manage ad privacy choices where required. When the “Privacy Options” entry appears, you can reopen it from the Cloud Shop's More menu.

App Store purchases

If in-app purchases become available, Apple processes payments through the App Store. The game receives verified purchase entitlements through StoreKit; it does not receive or store card details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple's data practices.
