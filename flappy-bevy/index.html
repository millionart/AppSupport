How to play

  • Tap to flap; hold to climb higher.
  • Use the pause button to resume play or change sound settings.
  • Collect matching foods to earn a talent choice.
  • High scores and collection progress are stored on your device. Removing the app removes this data.

Game Center

Signing in to Game Center is optional. Your highest score is sent to the leaderboard when you are signed in. Open the Game Center button from the start, pause, or game-over screen to see the leaderboard.
