FlipHello Privacy Policy

Updated: October 24, 2026

This policy applies to the FlipHello iOS app.

Contacts and local data

With your permission, FlipHello reads contact names, phone numbers, photos, and the existing Notes field in Apple Contacts to help you remember and organize people. It reads Notes to import per-contact ratings, tags, and memory cues, and to merge changes with existing text. Your edits are stored locally. Only when you choose Sync does FlipHello write ratings, tags, and memory cues to the corresponding contact's Notes field, along with any names or photos you edited. It reads the current Notes before writing and retains unrelated note content.

FlipHello has no account or developer-operated server for contact data. Contact names, phone numbers, photos, and Notes are not uploaded to the developer or included in advertising requests. The Google ad SDK's separate data practices are described below.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Changes you previously synced to iPhone Contacts remain there until you edit or delete them in Contacts.

Advertising

FlipHello uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When the app requests an ad, Google may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, ad activity and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's iOS data disclosure.

App Store purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. FlipHello checks purchase status to unlock features and does not receive your payment card details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
