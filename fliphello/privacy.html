FlipHello Privacy Policy

Updated: September 29, 2026

This policy applies to the FlipHello iOS app.

Contacts and local data

With your permission, FlipHello reads contact names, phone numbers, photos, and notes to help you remember and organize people. Ratings, tags, memory cues, and other edits are stored on your device. FlipHello has no account or developer-operated server for this data, and the developer does not receive your contacts. The app writes changes to iPhone Contacts only when you choose to sync.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Changes you previously synced to iPhone Contacts remain there until you edit or delete them in Contacts.

Advertising

FlipHello uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When the app requests an ad, Google may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, ad activity and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's iOS data disclosure.

App Store purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. FlipHello checks purchase status to unlock features and does not receive your payment card details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
