Animal Intelligence Company Privacy Policy

Updated: October 13, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS game Animal Intelligence Company.

Game progress

No game account or sign-in is required. Company progress, decisions, story unlocks, and sound settings are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a game server that receives these saves. Removing the app removes its locally stored game data.

Companies, characters, currencies, AI models, and data-trading events are fictional game content. The game does not train a real AI model or access your contacts, messages, photos, or private files for these mechanics.

Advertising

The game uses Google Mobile Ads to display optional rewarded ads. Google's SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, crash logs, and diagnostic and performance information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and service operation. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's app data disclosure guidance.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent where required. UMP may process approximate location, performance information, and interactions to provide consent choices. When “Ad Privacy Settings” appears in Help or the advertising screen, you can use it to reopen those choices. This version does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) through App Tracking Transparency.

App Store purchases

Apple processes the optional, non-consumable Ad-Free Restarts purchase. StoreKit supplies verified purchase entitlements so the game can unlock and restore the feature. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or upload purchase records to a developer-operated server. Apple handles App Store transactions under Apple's Privacy Policy.
